📝 Seller Profit-Sharing Agreement

You must sign this agreement before you can upload products.

  1. Revenue share. For every confirmed sale of your product, you receive 70% and DigiMarket (admin) keeps 30% of the listed price.
  2. How payment works. Customers pay DigiMarket. A sale counts only after DigiMarket confirms the payment. Your 70% share is then paid to your mobile money number on the payout schedule DigiMarket announces.
  3. Your content. You confirm that you own, or have the right to sell, everything you upload. Pirated, illegal or harmful content is not allowed.
  4. Prices. You set the price in RWF. The 30% is taken from that price, so customers pay only the listed price.
  5. Removal and refunds. DigiMarket may remove products that break these terms and may deduct the share of refunded or fraudulent sales from your earnings.
  6. Signing. Typing your full name and ticking the box below counts as your signature. The date and time are recorded.
